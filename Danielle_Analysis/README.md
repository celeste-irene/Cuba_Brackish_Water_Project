# Groundwater Quality Assessment
**Danielle Glikman**

This folder contains my groundwater quality assessment for Team 6's Cuba, New Mexico, water scarcity project.

The analysis uses groundwater data from Alamogordo, New Mexico, to examine uranium and arsenic concentrations across four groundwater well groups and compare them with EPA drinking water limits.

## Uranium Analysis

The uranium analysis compares groundwater concentrations with the EPA drinking water limit of **0.030 mg/L**.

All four well groups had uranium concentrations below the EPA limit, although the concentrations varied between groups.

## Arsenic Analysis

The arsenic analysis compares groundwater concentrations with the EPA drinking water limit of **0.010 mg/L**.

The graph shows how arsenic concentrations vary between groundwater well groups and helps illustrate why testing for multiple contaminants is important when evaluating groundwater quality.

## Connection to Cuba, New Mexico
Although the measurements come from Alamogordo, New Mexico, they help illustrate how groundwater quality can vary between wells. This is important for our project in Cuba because extracting and treating brackish groundwater may require removing contaminants such as uranium and arsenic.

Wells with higher contaminant concentrations may require more treatment, which could increase costs and produce more contaminated waste. Understanding existing groundwater quality before drilling or extraction is important for reducing pollution risks and protecting surrounding freshwater resources.
